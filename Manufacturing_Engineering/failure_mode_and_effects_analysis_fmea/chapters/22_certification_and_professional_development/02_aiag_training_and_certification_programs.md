## AIAG Training and Certification Programs


### Overview

The Automotive Industry Action Group (AIAG) is a nonprofit organization founded by North American automakers (originally Chrysler, Ford, and General Motors) that develops and maintains the Core Tools reference manuals used across the automotive supply chain — including FMEA, APQP, PPAP, SPC, MSA, and Control Plans. AIAG offers structured training and competency-based certification exams tied to these manuals, along with auditor certifications for IATF 16949. Since 2019, AIAG has co-developed FMEA content with the German VDA QMC, producing the harmonized **AIAG & VDA FMEA Handbook**, which has largely superseded the legacy standalone AIAG FMEA 4th Edition manual for new training, though the 4th Edition certification exam remains referenced in some current offerings. ### AIAG Core Tools and Their Relationship to FMEA

**Key Points**

- AIAG's "Core Tools" are five interrelated automotive quality methodologies: APQP (Advanced Product Quality Planning), PPAP (Production Part Approval Process), FMEA, MSA (Measurement Systems Analysis), and SPC (Statistical Process Control).
- APQP is described by AIAG as a guiding force for other tools such as FMEA, MSA, and SPC, and is positioned as one of the most high-level Core Tools, meaning FMEA training is often sequenced after or alongside APQP training in a practitioner's development path. [aiag](https://aiag.org/training)
- Control Plans play a role in the success of other tools such as FMEA and SPC, reflecting how the DFMEA/PFMEA outputs feed directly into control plan development. [aiag](https://aiag.org/training)
- IATF 16949 (the automotive quality management system standard) references these Core Tools as expected methodologies, and internal and supplier auditor competency requirements defined in IATF 16949 Section 7.2.3 and 7.2.4 encourage participants to complete the AIAG Core Tools Self-Assessment (CTSA) to assess proficiency in the core tools prior to attending auditor training. [aiag](https://aiag.org/training)

### FMEA-Specific Training Courses

AIAG offers FMEA training at multiple depths, generally aligned with the harmonized AIAG & VDA FMEA Handbook's 7-Step Approach.

| Course | Format | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Understanding and Implementing FMEA | Classroom & Live Virtual | Teaches the skills to understand and use FMEAs and the concept of risk reduction and defect prevention, including developing and maintaining design and process FMEAs |
| AIAG & VDA Design FMEA: Understanding and Implementing | Live Virtual | Assists the practitioner in execution of Design FMEA using the new AIAG & VDA FMEA Handbook, applying the 7-Step Approach and developing an organizational adoption plan |
| AIAG & VDA Process FMEA: Understanding and Implementing with Control Plans | Classroom & Live Virtual | Covers execution of PFMEA using the 7-Step Approach, with linkage to Cost of Poor Quality (CoPQ) |
| Reverse FMEA for Practitioners | Live Virtual (1-day) | Addresses the OEM-mandated Reverse FMEA requirement, walking through fundamentals and a step-by-step completed example framed as a process audit activity |
| FMEA Certification Exam | Live Virtual (proctored) | Competency qualification verifying an individual's competency in FMEA techniques as defined in AIAG's FMEA reference manual, with ability to apply and communicate the methodology to support risk identification and management |
| AIAG and VDA Process FMEA Certification Exam | Live Virtual (proctored) | Focuses specifically on PFMEA as defined in the AIAG & VDA FMEA Handbook Chapter 3, confirming ability to apply and communicate PFMEA methodology |

### Certification Exam Format and Logistics

**Key Points**

- FMEA-related certification exams are delivered as online, proctored exams requiring a device capable of streaming video, which is retained to determine any testing irregularities; candidates must keep cameras active throughout. [aiag](https://aiag.org/training)
- Passing requires a Multiple-Choice knowledge test, after which a certificate of qualification is issued (this format is used in the parallel VDA QMC delivery channel for the harmonized handbook). [vda](https://webshop.vda.de/Training/442_26_01_fmea_according_to_aiag_vda_basics)
- Some third-party-delivered versions of the harmonized FMEA courses use tiered qualification: foundation, practitioner, and expert levels, with the foundation level requiring passing a qualification exam. [Unverified: this tiered structure is documented by third-party training providers licensed to teach AIAG & VDA content, not confirmed as AIAG's own internal tier naming — verify against AIAG's direct catalog if a specific tier name matters for compliance purposes.]
- Certification exams for other Core Tools (APQP/PPAP, MSA, SPC) follow the same proctored-exam pattern and are frequently bundled into the same testing week as the FMEA exam.

### Related Auditor and Systems Training Relevant to FMEA Integration

**Key Points**

- The AIAG IATF 16949:2016 Lead Auditor Training with AIAG Supplier Auditor Certification is described as the highest level of auditor training available to automotive suppliers, combining AIAG Supplier Auditor Certification with ISO 9001:2015 Lead Auditor Training, and requires familiarity with Core Tools including FMEA to audit conformance effectively. [aiag](https://aiag.org/training)
- The AIAG IATF 16949:2016 Internal Auditor Training is a 2-day course for new and experienced internal auditors performing internal quality audits to IATF 16949:2016, including case study activities on industry-recognized auditing techniques, where FMEA documentation review is a common audit focus area. [aiag](https://aiag.org/training)
- VDA 6.3 process auditor qualification (delivered via VDA QMC, AIAG's harmonization partner) also touches FMEA indirectly, since the VDA 6.3 process approach requires identifying risks and potentials along the supply chain, which draws on PFMEA outputs during audits. [aiag](https://aiag.org/training)

### Delivery Formats

**Key Points**

- **Classroom (in-person)**: Typically held at AIAG's Southfield, MI headquarters or partner training centers; includes hands-on group exercises and case studies.
- **Live Virtual Training**: Delivered via video conferencing platforms (e.g., Zoom), with camera-on requirements for both instruction and proctored exams.
- **Hybrid/Blended Learning** (used in the parallel VDA QMC delivery model): combines an online pre-course quiz to gauge experience level, classroom training with integrated individual and group tasks, and a follow-up webinar roughly eight weeks after attendance to address open questions. [vda](https://webshop.vda.de/Training/442_26_01_fmea_according_to_aiag_vda_basics)[vda](https://webshop.vda.de/Training/442_24_01_fmea_according_to_aiag_vda_basics)
- **On-Demand/eLearning**: Some certification exams (e.g., FMEA Certification Exam, MSA Certification Exam, SPC Certification Exam) are listed with recurring or on-demand dates, allowing flexible scheduling independent of classroom cohorts.

### Prerequisites and Target Audience

**Key Points**

- Foundation-level FMEA courses generally assume no formal prerequisite but recommend 1–2 years of product application or manufacturing process knowledge for meaningful engagement.
- Target roles typically include: design engineers, process/manufacturing engineers, new product development (NPD) personnel, quality engineers, and engineering managers responsible for maintaining DFMEAs/PFMEAs.
- Auditor-track certifications (e.g., IATF 16949 Lead Auditor) often list the AIAG Core Tools Self-Assessment (CTSA) as a recommended readiness check before enrollment, rather than a strict gatekeeping prerequisite.

### Cost and Access Considerations

**Key Points**

- AIAG offers both knowledge and application core tools courses to help participants become certified with globally recognized credentials in FMEA, with example overview-level pricing historically differentiated by AIAG membership status (e.g., $175 for members versus $225 for non-members for a half-day FMEA Overview session). - Parallel VDA QMC-delivered harmonized FMEA courses (relevant for organizations operating in both AIAG and German/European VDA supply chains) are priced separately in EUR, with a basic two-day live online course listed around 950 EUR excluding tax, with early-bird discounts available. [vda](https://webshop.vda.de/Training/442_26_01_fmea_according_to_aiag_vda_basics)
- Organizations sourcing to both North American OEMs and European (VDA-aligned) OEMs often need staff trained in both the AIAG lineage and the fully harmonized AIAG & VDA Handbook to satisfy customer-specific requirements from each region.

### Certification Value and Renewal

**Key Points**

- Certifications tied to standards revisions (e.g., IATF 16949 rule editions, VDA 6.3 editions) typically require **upgrade or transition training** when the underlying reference manual or rule set changes — for example, a dedicated VDA 6.3 upgrade course exists specifically to teach the changes from the 2016 edition to the 2023 edition of the VDA 6.3 questionnaire and assessment scheme. [aiag](https://aiag.org/training)
- Auditor-level certifications (as opposed to knowledge-only certificates) generally carry renewal cycles; the IATF 16949 Lead Auditor with Supplier Auditor Recertification course renews a prior certification for another 3-year period, illustrating the periodic recertification pattern common across AIAG's auditor credentials. [aiag](https://aiag.org/training)
- A plain "Certificate of Qualification" from a knowledge-test-based FMEA course is distinct from — and generally less rigorous than — a formal auditor certification; the former demonstrates methodology competency, while the latter demonstrates ability to audit others' conformance to it.

### Practical Guidance for Selecting a Training Path

**Example**

A process engineer newly assigned PFMEA responsibility at a Tier 1 automotive supplier would typically follow this path: (1) complete APQP Overview to understand where PFMEA fits in the product development timeline, (2) take AIAG & VDA Process FMEA: Understanding and Implementing with Control Plans to learn the 7-Step Approach and its link to control plan generation, (3) sit the AIAG and VDA Process FMEA Certification Exam to obtain a portable competency credential, and (4) periodically attend Reverse FMEA for Practitioners if their OEM customers mandate reverse FMEA reviews as part of supplier quality assurance documentation.
