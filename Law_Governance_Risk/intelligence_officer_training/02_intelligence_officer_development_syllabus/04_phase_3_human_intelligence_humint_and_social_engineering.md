## Phase 3 — Human Intelligence (HUMINT) and Social Engineering


**Purpose:** The core of field intelligence work. Running people.

### Module 3.1 — Rapport and Elicitation

**Topics:** The science of liking, trust acceleration, mirroring, active listening as extraction, elicitation techniques (flattery, bracketing, false statements, quid pro quo)

**Skills:**

- Building genuine-feeling rapport with strangers rapidly
- Extracting specific information without the subject realizing
- Identifying what a person wants and using it as leverage for relationship

**Resources:**

- _Never Split the Difference_ — Chris Voss (negotiation as a HUMINT skill)
- _How to Win Friends and Influence People_ — Carnegie (foundational, underrated)
- _The Like Switch_ — Jack Schafer (written by former FBI behavioral analyst)
- _Spy the Lie_ — Philip Houston (former CIA)
- _What Every Body Is Saying_ — Joe Navarro (former FBI nonverbal analyst)

---

### Module 3.2 — Agent Recruitment and Management

**Topics:** The MICE framework (Money, Ideology, Compromise, Ego) and its modern extensions, spotting, assessing, developing, and recruiting sources, managing long-term agent relationships, handling agent psychological deterioration

**Skills:**

- Identifying recruitment targets and their vulnerability profiles
- Pitching a recruitment without triggering alarm
- Maintaining a source's motivation and security over time

**Resources:**

- _The Spy's Son_ — Bryan Denson (recruitment from both sides)
- _By Way of Deception_ — Victor Ostrovsky [disputed, read critically, still instructive]
- _The Main Enemy_ — Milt Bearden & James Risen
- _Intelligence: From Secrets to Policy_ — Mark Lowenthal (academic framework)

---

### Module 3.3 — Deception and Cover

**Topics:** Identity construction, legend building, cover story architecture, maintaining cover under direct questioning, the psychology of sustained deception

**Skills:**

- Building a false identity that survives casual and structured scrutiny
- Staying in cover under stress
- Detecting when your cover is being probed

**Resources:**

- _The Art of Deception_ — Kevin Mitnick (technical social engineering, principles transfer)
- _Influence_ — Robert Cialdini (the mechanics of how people are persuaded)
- _Catch Me If You Can_ — Frank Abagnale (cover maintenance as lived practice)
- Academic: Paul Ekman's research on deception detection (papers available via ResearchGate)
