## Phase 4 — Tradecraft


**Purpose:** The technical vocabulary of fieldwork. These are procedures, not instincts.

### Module 4.1 — Surveillance and Counter-Surveillance

**Topics:** Foot and vehicle surveillance, surveillance detection routes (SDRs), identifying fixed and mobile surveillance, the ABC surveillance team model, dry cleaning techniques

**Skills:**

- Running an SDR without appearing to
- Identifying surveillance before it becomes a threat
- Understanding how surveillance teams think and operate

**Resources:**

- _Surveillance Zone_ — Barry Davies
- _The Spy Who Came in from the Cold_ — le Carré (fiction, but tradecraft-accurate)
- _Trading Secrets_ — Peter Earnest (International Spy Museum curator, former CIA)
- Open-source: CIA's _Tradecraft Primer_ (declassified, available via CIA FOIA reading room)

---

### Module 4.2 — Covert Communication

**Topics:** Dead drops, brush passes, signal sites, one-time pads, steganography basics, secure digital communication, operational security (OPSEC)

**Skills:**

- Passing material without direct contact
- Communicating in ways that deny association
- Digital hygiene at an operational level

**Resources:**

- _Spycraft_ — Robert Wallace & H. Keith Melton (historical but technically rich)
- _The Art of Invisibility_ — Kevin Mitnick (digital OPSEC)
- EFF's Surveillance Self-Defense guide (free, technically current)
- _No Place to Hide_ — Glenn Greenwald (understanding what surveillance captures)

---

### Module 4.3 — Physical and Environmental Awareness

**Topics:** Route planning and escape routes, identifying choke points, safe house selection criteria, border and checkpoint behavior, search resistance

**Skills:**

- Moving through an environment with an operational mindset
- Selecting and using physical space to your advantage
- Behaving naturally at border crossings and checkpoints

**Resources:**

- _SAS Survival Handbook_ — John Wiseman (environmental awareness foundation)
- _The Operator_ — Robert O'Neill (mindset under operational pressure)
- _The Escape Artist_ — Jonathan Freedland (evasion thinking)
