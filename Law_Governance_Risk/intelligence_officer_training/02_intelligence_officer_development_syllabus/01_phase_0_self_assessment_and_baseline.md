## Phase 0 — Self-Assessment and Baseline


**Purpose:** Honest audit before investment. Agencies screen out people who don't know themselves.

### Topics

- Psychological self-profiling: Big Five, stress response patterns, attachment style under pressure
- Identifying exploitable personal vulnerabilities (financial, relational, ideological)
- Cognitive baseline: memory, pattern recognition, linguistic aptitude
- Ethical threshold mapping — where are your actual limits, not your imagined ones

### Skills

- Sustained self-observation without self-flattery
- Identifying your own tells and behavioral leakage
- Honest risk tolerance assessment

### Resources

- _The Gifted Adult_ — Mary-Elaine Jacobsen (self-assessment framework)
- _Thinking, Fast and Slow_ — Kahneman (cognitive bias baseline)
- CIA's publicly released _Psychology of Intelligence Analysis_ — Richards Heuer (free PDF, declassified)
- _The Body Keeps the Score_ — van der Kolk (understanding your own stress physiology)
