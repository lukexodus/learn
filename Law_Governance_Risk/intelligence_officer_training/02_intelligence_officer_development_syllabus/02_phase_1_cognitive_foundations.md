## Phase 1 — Cognitive Foundations


**Purpose:** Build the mental infrastructure everything else runs on.

### Module 1.1 — Memory and Attention

**Topics:** Working memory expansion, chunking, method of loci, selective attention under distraction, prospective memory

**Skills:**

- Memorizing names, faces, and details rapidly
- Retaining cover story details under social pressure
- Noticing environmental anomalies automatically

**Resources:**

- _Moonwalking with Einstein_ — Joshua Foer
- _The Memory Book_ — Lorayne & Lucas
- Dual N-Back training (free, cognitively validated)

---

### Module 1.2 — Analytical Thinking

**Topics:** Structured analytic techniques (SATs), hypothesis generation, confirmation bias mitigation, red teaming your own conclusions

**Skills:**

- Analysis of Competing Hypotheses (ACH)
- Distinguishing signal from noise in ambiguous information
- Writing intelligence-style assessments: bottom-line-up-front, sourced, hedged

**Resources:**

- _Psychology of Intelligence Analysis_ — Heuer (declassified, free)
- _Structured Analytic Techniques for Intelligence Analysis_ — Heuer & Pherson
- _The Intelligence Trap_ — David Robson

---

### Module 1.3 — Pattern Recognition and Situational Awareness

**Topics:** Baseline behavior in environments, anomaly detection, Kim's Game, observational frameworks (Cooper Color Code)

**Skills:**

- Establishing environmental baselines rapidly
- Detecting surveillance
- Retaining detailed spatial and behavioral information

**Resources:**

- _Left of Bang_ — Patrick Van Horne (Marine threat assessment, civilian applicable)
- _The Gift of Fear_ — Gavin de Becker
- Kim's Game practice (daily observational drills — no purchase required)
