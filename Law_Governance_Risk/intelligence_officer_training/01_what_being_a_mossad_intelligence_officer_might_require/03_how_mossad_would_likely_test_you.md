### Phase 1 — Passive Observation Before You Know You're Being Assessed

Multiple accounts (Ostrovsky, journalistic sources) suggest Mossad identifies candidates long before approaching them — through military performance reviews, university networks, and existing officer recommendations. The assessment may have already begun.

**What they're watching:** consistency between public and private behavior, how you handle failure, who you trust with what, whether your values match your actions.

### Phase 2 — Initial Approach and Vetting

The approach is typically indirect — a conversation that doesn't announce itself as recruitment. [Inference: this serves as a live test of your observational awareness and discretion. Do you notice? Do you talk about it?]

Background investigation at this stage is exhaustive: financial records, relationships, foreign contacts, psychological history, any ideological affiliations.

### Phase 3 — Psychological and Cognitive Assessment

Documented in CIA selection (publicly) and [Inference applied to Mossad]:

- **Structured stress interviews** — interviewers are deliberately destabilizing. The test is not your answers but your regulation
- **Cognitive battery** — pattern recognition, memory under distraction, language aptitude
- **Personality profiling** — likely including tools analogous to MMPI or similar clinical instruments
- **Scenario judgment tests** — ethical dilemmas with no clean answer, assessing how you reason under conflict

### Phase 4 — Field Exercises

Ostrovsky describes exercises that include:

- **Surveillance detection routes** — can you identify if you're being followed?
- **Cold approach tasks** — approach a stranger, build rapport, extract specific information, do not reveal your purpose
- **Cover stress testing** — sustain a false identity under direct, hostile questioning
- **Judgment under time pressure** — incomplete information, real consequences for the exercise, no clean options

### Phase 5 — Psychological Endurance

Extended isolation, sleep disruption, sustained performance requirements. Not to break you, but to see who you are when the performative layer falls away.
