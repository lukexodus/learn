## The Honest Part Most Sources Skip


The selection rate is extremely low. Published estimates for analogous agencies (CIA's Directorate of Operations, MI6) run below 1–2% of applicants reaching operational deployment.

The work is structurally corrosive to ordinary life. Ostrovsky, Bergman, and others document: failed marriages, estrangement from family, difficulty trusting anyone, and the particular grief of successes you can never speak of.

The moral weight is real. Ronen Bergman's _Rise and Kill First_ — the most rigorously sourced account of Mossad operations — documents operations where officers had to personally assess whether a specific human being should die based on incomplete intelligence. Those decisions stay with people.
