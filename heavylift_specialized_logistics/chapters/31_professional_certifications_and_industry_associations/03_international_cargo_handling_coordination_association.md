## International Cargo Handling Coordination Association


### Overview

The International Cargo Handling Coordination Association (ICHCA) is an independent, not-for-profit organisation, established in 1952, dedicated to improving the safety, productivity and efficiency of cargo handling and movement worldwide. Headquartered in the UK, ICHCA serves a global network of members and contacts in the cargo handling sector, and is relevant to heavy-lift and specialized logistics practice primarily through its work on cargo securing standards, safety best practice, and its consultative role with key international regulatory bodies governing goods movement.

---

### History and Legal Structure

**Key Points**

- ICHCA was established in 1952 with one simple goal: to help disseminate the best practice and lessons learned from logistics operations throughout the industry.
- ICHCA International Limited is a membership organisation originally established in 1952 and incorporated in 2002 — indicating the association operated for roughly five decades before adopting its current formal corporate/legal structure.
- ICHCA is described as an independent, non-political and non-profit making association, dedicated to the promotion of efficiency and economy in the handling and movement of goods, from origin to destination, by all modes, and at all phases of the national and international transportation chain.

---

### Mission and Scope

**Key Points**

- ICHCA remains dedicated to improving the productivity, safety, efficiency, security and sustainability of cargo handling across the world — a five-part mission scope (productivity, safety, efficiency, security, sustainability) that has broadened somewhat over the organisation's history from an initial primary focus on safety, productivity, and efficiency alone.
- The association's object is described as promoting efficiency in the movement of goods by all modes of transport and at all phases of the transport chain, achieved substantially through the work of its Panels of industry experts.
- ICHCA is positioned as the leading NGO for the global cargo handling industry, working closely with international bodies and heavily participating in the work of The Cargo Integrity Group, which builds on work commenced by a grouping of industry associations to raise awareness and greater uptake of the CTU Code (Code of Practice for Packing of Cargo Transport Units) — directly relevant to heavy-lift and specialized cargo operators responsible for load securing and cargo transport unit packing.

---

### Membership Structure

**Key Points**

- ICHCA has an influential and international membership in more than 80 countries, with a network of 11 National Sections putting members in direct contact with industry colleagues around the world.
- ICHCA International operates through a series of autonomous national and regional chapters — including ICHCA Australia, ICHCA Japan, and ICHCA Canarias/Africa (CARC) — plus Correspondence and Working Groups, together providing a focal point for informing, educating, networking, shaping, and sharing industry views to improve knowledge and best practice across the global cargo chain.
- ICHCA's membership represents the largest cross section of senior corporate executives, leading consultants, academics, and authorities in the world of cargo handling and transportation, functioning as an international resource centre providing management information and the exchange of technical data relating to methods and techniques in the industry.
- Membership tiers include Corporate, Premium, Individual, and Licensed Membership categories:
  - **Premium Members** receive one free place at every ICHCA event as standard, benefit from a specially discounted rate for further delegates at all ICHCA events, and receive ten votes, alongside the advantages of Corporate Membership and broad industry exposure through opportunities to contribute advertisements and guest editorials via ICHCA's communication channels.
  - **Individual Members** are issued an invoice in their own name and must be registered at a private address to qualify.
  - **Licensed Members** are organisations with multiple members, companies, or operational sites — such as a global corporation, association, or club — where a licence fee per member/company/site is paid in addition to the corporate membership fee.
- Member-reported value themes include the industry experience and resource materials available through membership, and the opportunity to network with people from around the world, with many problems described as easily solvable by contacting a fellow member directly — reflecting the association's core function as a peer-to-peer industry knowledge and problem-solving network.

**Key Points**

- As the leading NGO for the global cargo handling industry, ICHCA works closely with the IMO, ILO, UNECE, and other UN bodies, giving it a direct channel into the formation of international regulations and legislation affecting cargo handling and movement.
- ICHCA has, for many years, enjoyed non-governmental (NGO) consultative status with a number of intergovernmental agencies whose programme of work impacts the international cargo handling and transportation industry — chief among these being the IMO, UNCTAD, the UN Economic and Social Councils (ECOSOC), particularly for Europe (UNECE) and Asia Pacific (ESCAP), the European Council of Ministers of Transport (ECMT), the European Commission (EC), and the OECD.
- ICHCA also maintains observer and liaison status with a number of international industry bodies, including the International Standards Organisation (ISO), European Standards Organisation (CEN), International Chamber of Shipping (ICS), and International Chamber of Commerce (ICC), and takes an active role in several international industry fora.
- ICHCA has made an important contribution to the debate on a wide range of industry issues around the world and, in many cases, has had a direct effect on the formation of international regulations and legislation — this representative function is carried out by staff of the International Secretariat as well as ICHCA Panel members and others.

### Panels, Publications, and Technical Resources

**Key Points**

- One of the primary mechanisms through which ICHCA fulfils its mission is the work of its Panels of industry experts — for example, the Safety Panel is made up of approximately 40 acknowledged experts in the ports and related industries who specialise in all aspects of cargo as well as personnel, with a remit covering legal issues, safe working practices, equipment, and training; the Safety Panel meets three times a year at various global venues, debating issues and attending IMO, ISO, BSI, and similar standards-body sessions to represent ICHCA's views.
- ICHCA International produces a range of specialist publications related to cargo handling safety, security, sustainability, efficiency, and legislative compliance, alongside a monthly newsletter (ICHCA Insights) available to subscribers.
- Historical ICHCA publication offerings for members have included a bimonthly magazine with the latest cargo handling news, a "who's who" in the industry, a buyer's guide, an annual review of the year's major events, and individual publications generated from the research and study activities of ICHCA's sub-committees on a wide variety of issues and technical matters.
- Members gain access to the ICHCA database at the International Secretariat for answering technical queries — an information pool supplemented by the expertise held amongst the membership itself, with material from all sources submitted by members welcomed and incorporated.
- Membership benefits also include discounted training courses — specifically noted examples include IMDG Code (International Maritime Dangerous Goods Code) compliance training and proper CTU (Cargo Transport Unit) packing training — alongside discounts on all IMO publications, both of direct practical relevance to specialized/heavy cargo handling and securing operations.

### Relevance to Heavy-Lift and Specialized Logistics Practice

**Key Points**

- ICHCA's active role in promoting the CTU Code (Code of Practice for Packing of Cargo Transport Units) through The Cargo Integrity Group is directly relevant to heavy-lift and project-cargo operators, since proper packing, securing, and load distribution within cargo transport units is a core technical concern for oversized and heavy cargo movement across multimodal supply chains.
- ICHCA functions as a unique cross-sector networking body — getting all stakeholders round the table to resolve together issues that affect business for everyone in the cargo chain — which is distinct from both NCCCO's individual-personnel certification role and SC&RA's more transport/rigging-industry-specific advocacy role (both covered elsewhere in this chapter): ICHCA's scope is broader, covering cargo handling across all modes and all phases of the transport chain, rather than being focused specifically on crane/rigging personnel or specialized heavy-haul carriers.
- ICHCA's NGO consultative status with bodies such as the IMO gives the association — and by extension its member companies — a channel to influence international regulation directly relevant to cargo handling safety and cargo securing standards that heavy-lift and specialized logistics operators must comply with.
- **Behavior may vary**: [Behavioral disclaimer] Membership fee structures, panel compositions, publication offerings, and specific regulatory-body relationships are subject to change over time; the details above should be verified directly against ICHCA's current website (ichca.com) before being used for membership decisions or compliance planning.
